THE STORY ARC

Explore the four key emotional phases of Niran and Cheewa's journey across all 30 chapters.

Chapters 1–6

First Encounters & Niran's Crisis

Niran meets Cheewa, becomes curious about him, and simultaneously struggles with losing his passion for judo. The unexpected bus collision sparks an entire chain of gravitational events.

Chapters 7–14

Gravity / Growing Connection

The Planetarium, astronomy, volunteer camp, judo lessons, and Science Building mystery bring Niran and Cheewa much closer together in orbit.

Chapters 15–23

Feelings + Mystery + Conflict

Their relationship becomes increasingly obvious while the Aoy/Science Building conflict reaches its final resolution and exoneration.

Chapters 24–30

Family, Future & Relationship

Niran's family problems begin healing, Cheewa's own past is explored, Niran learns to reconsider his future, and their relationship becomes official under the night sky.

THE UNIVERSE OF SOULS

CHARACTERS (14 PROFILES)

Choose a category below to explore specific character groups.

Main Protagonist

1. Pieng-Niran (Niran)

Niran is a student and a talented judo athlete who is beginning to lose his passion for the sport. He is more serious and practical than Cheewa, but he gradually becomes more open to new experiences because of him. His relationship with Cheewa is one of the central elements of the novel.

Important Traits:
  • Judo athlete
  • Responsible and straightforward
  • Initially uncertain about his future
  • Protective of people close to him
  • Becomes increasingly comfortable with Cheewa
  • Has complicated family circumstances
Main Protagonist / Love Interest

2. Parncheewa "Cheewa"

Cheewa is Niran's younger schoolmate. He is strongly interested in astronomy, belongs to the Astronomy Club, and is known for being somewhat unusual compared with other students. He is intelligent, calm, curious, and communicates through astronomy and scientific ideas.

Important Traits:
  • Astronomy enthusiast & Astronomy Club member
  • Student council member
  • Learns judo from Niran; later plays basketball & drums
  • Comes from a wealthy family
  • Very protective of Niran (explicitly tells Niran he wanted to protect him)
Niran's Close Friend / Comic Relief

3. Hemarat

Hemarat is one of Niran's closest friends and frequently teases Niran about Cheewa. Observant and often the first person to notice the growing connection between Niran and Cheewa. Also participates in the Science Building investigation.

Niran's Friend Group

4. Thara

Thara is part of Niran's close friend group alongside Hemarat and Thipok. He often participates in conversations, school activities, and the investigation surrounding the fake Science Building ghost.

Niran's Friend / Music Club

5. Thipok

Thipok is another member of Niran's friend group involved in gathering photographic and video evidence for the Science Building investigation. Later becomes involved with the Music Club and asks Cheewa to help with a performance.

Cheewa's Close Friend / Astronomy Club

6. Jomyut

Jomyut is one of the Astronomy Club members and is very close to Cheewa. Intelligent, glasses-wearing, and science-oriented. He also becomes part of Niran's wider friend group and frequently jokes about Niran and Cheewa's interactions.

Cheewa's Older Brother

7. Cheewin

Cheewin is Cheewa's older brother. He is involved in taking care of Cheewa and occasionally interacts with Niran and their friends. Establishes that Cheewa's family is wealthy and drives him to school.

Antagonist / Schoolmate

8. Aoy

Aoy has an ongoing conflict with Niran, repeatedly believing Niran is responsible for things happening to him. Their conflict connects to the fake Science Building ghost mystery before evidence clears Niran.

Aoy's Associate

9. Naw

Naw is connected to Aoy and the students responsible for the Science Building incidents. During the investigation, Naw acts as a decoy and secretly provides information about group movements.

School Authority / Disciplinarian

10. Professor Suchart

Professor Suchart handles disciplinary matters at school. Involved in Niran and Aoy's conflicts and the Science Building incident. Strict, but considers evidence before making decisions.

Judo Coach

11. Coach Ong

Coach Ong is connected to Niran's judo career and is also associated with the national judo council. Plays an important role in Niran's athletic life and eventually encourages him to take time to recover and think about his future.

Parents & Family (12–14)

12–14. Family Members

Niran's Father: Struggles with Niran over his judo future; bond gradually heals.
Niran's Mother: Lives separately; Niran gradually gains better understanding of her.
Cheewa's Parents: Financially comfortable, providing Cheewa opportunities to explore diverse interests.
